# L11-L16: RD68041, an MC68040, in LiteX

RD68041 (`Inputs/RD68041`) is an MC68040, or an MC68LC040 with its `FPU`
parameter at 0. It takes the same path as RD68031:
- a bridge on the L1 bench;
- a LiteX CPU, then the BIOS and bare metal in litex_sim;
- the Wukong;
- Linux, first with RD68031's userspace, then with buildroot built for the 68040.

RD68031 keeps working throughout; it is still the default everywhere.

```sh
scripts/sync_pythondata_rd68041.sh        # ../pythondata-cpu-rd68041, patches/RD68041 applied
make -C sim/bench check CPU=rd68041 [FPU=0]
make -C sim/litex check bios CPU=rd68041 [VARIANT=standard+fpu]
sim/litex/l4_test.py --cpu-type rd68041 --variant standard+fpu [--sdram]
targets/qmtech_wukong.py --cpu-type rd68041 --cpu-variant standard+fpu \
    --uart-baudrate 1000000 --with-ethernet --with-sdcard \
    --output-dir build/qmtech_wukong_rd68041_25m_fpu_1m_io \
    --vivado-place-directive ExtraTimingOpt \
    --vivado-post-place-phys-opt-directive AggressiveExplore \
    --vivado-route-directive Explore --build
buildroot/build.sh 040-fpu                # or 040-nofpu: ~/LITEX-68K/build/buildroot-040-<v>
sim/litex/l7_test.py --cpu-type rd68041 --login --io --no-sd \
    --serial /dev/ttyUSB0 --speed 1000000 \
    --linux-dir ~/LITEX-68K/build/buildroot-040-fpu/images \
    --build-dir build/qmtech_wukong_rd68041_25m_fpu_1m_io
```

## The variants

| `--cpu-variant` | The part | `FPU` | Software |
|---|---|---|---|
| `standard` | MC68LC040 | 0 | `-mcpu=68040 -msoft-float`; every FP instruction takes F-line with a format $4 frame |
| `standard+fpu` | MC68040 | 1 | `-mcpu=68040`; the instructions the 040 leaves to software trap for the M68040FPSP (vector 11, format $2) |

There is no `minimal`: the caches are fixed in the RTL and CACR turns them
on.

## The bridge (L11)

`litex_68k/m68040_bus.py`, `M68040ToFIFOToSoC`, written from the MC68040
manual's section 7. It is the processor's side of a FIFO bridge, like L6's.
`litex_68k/m68k_fifo.py` has the SoC's side and the FIFOs, now shared by
both CPUs.

- **Transfers:**
  - TS is remembered when it comes. The attributes are read from the bus,
    where they stay until the last TA.
  - Reads wait for their answer.
  - A line read is answered as a burst of four TAs, starting at A3-A2 and
    wrapping (UM 7.4.2). QuadraFPGA hands a line out in the order 0-3; the
    manual asks for the wrap.
  - A line write is taken one long word a clock, then posted as one 128-bit
    request. The request format gained a "wide" form (128 bits of data, 16
    byte enables) for it; the 030 keeps its narrow one.
  - Other writes are posted outside `io_regions` and wait inside.
- **Termination:**
  - TCI on every read in `io_regions`.
  - TEA for errors and for the breakpoint acknowledge.
  - AVEC with TA for interrupt acknowledges.
  - No retry, no TBI.
- **The only master:**
  - BG is tied low and SC is 00: nothing snoops.
  - The core's TS, BB, A, TT, R/W and SIZ are fed back to its inputs, as
    RD68041's own rd-virt does.
- **Main RAM:** a 128-bit LiteDRAM native port, exactly a 040 line.
- **The bench:** `sim/bench`'s `CPU=rd68041`, with `programs040/bustest.S`:
  - every lane, and locked transfers;
  - access errors and autovectors;
  - TCI, cachable and serialized;
  - a line read in wrap order, a copyback push, and MOVE16.
- **The bench's verdict:** its pass value is $0040600D, not 0. A deliberately
  broken bridge, with no wrap, once ran astray and reached crt0's end with 0.
- **`+trace`** prints every transfer.

## The CPU class and the software (L12, L13)

`litex_68k/cpu/rd68041/`:
- **The mem_map and the IRQs:** the same mem_map and IRQ CSRs as RD68031,
  IPL 2 autovectored.
- **`crt0.S`:**
  - DTT0 makes the I/O region ($8000_0000 up) noncachable and serialized.
  - Everything else is the MMU-off default, cachable write-through (UM 3.1.2).
  - Then CINVA, and CACR DE and IE.
- **`system.h`:** CINVA IC and CPUSHA DC for the flushes.

`sim/litex/cputest040/` is cputest for the 040. It checks:
- the 040's exception frames: format $2 for CHK, TRAPV, trace and address
  errors, $7 for access errors (SSW, FA);
- the instruction cache, stale until CINV;
- 4 KiB pages through the three table levels, behind copyback, noncachable,
  write-protected and invalid aliases;
- U/M, PTEST/MMUSR and TTR hits;
- the FPU: FSAVE null, idle and unimplemented frames (version $41), FSIN and
  FMOVECR trapping, an enabled divide by zero;
- on the LC040, F-line with format $4.

One expectation was wrong at first. A noncachable read of a line that is
dirty in the copyback cache returned the new value. That is right: a
cache-inhibited access pushes a matching dirty line first (UM 4.3.2).

## The Wukong (L14)

25 MHz, from an MMCM of its own; the SoC is at 100 MHz. Ethernet and SD,
the L2 cache off, L10's directives.

| | WNS | LUTs | FFs | BRAM tiles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MC68040 | +0.601 ns | 35,927 (57 %) | 14,620 | 42.5 |
| MC68LC040 | +0.348 ns | 27,547 (43 %) | | |

The bitstreams are in `~/LITEX-68K/bitstreams/wukong-rd68041-{fpu,nofpu}-eth-sd-25m-p1`.

On the board, the MC68040:
- memspeed 10.3/9.7 MiB/s (RD68031 at 30 MHz: 14.1/11.7);
- the BIOS's test (L3) and cputest040 (L4, 97 checks) pass.

## Linux (L15, L16)

- **The kernel:** one kernel for both the 030 and the 040
  (`linux/litex-rd68031.config` has M68040 beside M68030, with the FPSP). The
  68040 buildroots have kernels for the 040 alone (`linux-040.config`).
- **litexboot:**
  - it reports `CPU_68040` and `MMU_68040`, and `FPU_68040` or none;
  - its FNOP probe takes the LC040's format $4 frame, whose PC is already past
    the instruction;
  - it leaves the 040 as out of reset: CPUSHA, CACR 0, CINVA, the TTRs
    cleared.
- **`l7_test.py --cpu-type rd68041`:** the cpuinfo and litexboot's FPU line
  follow the CPU.

### The bug L15 found in RD68041

**The symptom:**
- With the glibc userspace, init died of SIGSEGV on its first `fopen64`, and
  the kernel panicked. This happened on the board and in litex_sim alike.
- musl's userspace booted.
- The FPSP worked from user mode: a static test of FINTRZ, FMOVECR, FSIN and
  FETOX was exact.
- glibc's busybox ran and forked under musl's system.

**The trace:**
- `sim/litex/trace/rd68041_trace.sv`, built in with `RD68041_TRACE=1`, prints:
  - user-mode boundaries and stores;
  - the exception frames;
  - the instruction ATC's fills and flushes.
- It showed that both faults had correct frames:
  1. SSW $0502, FA $C0135FF8.
  2. SSW $0502, FA $C0136000.
- Between them, the fetch unit had gone on past the first fault to the next
  page. It searched the tables there and left a not-resident ATC entry
  (UM 3.2.2.3) for $C0136000.

**How Linux took it:**
1. Linux's handler mapped both pages (fault-around) and flushed the first
   page's entry only.
2. `MOVE.L 12(SP),-(SP)` at $C0135FFE, across the two pages, then faulted on
   the stale entry.
3. Linux's PTEST now found the page resident, so it took the fault for a
   protection violation.

**The fix:** `patches/RD68041` 0001.
- The fetch unit stops after an ATC fault until the next redirect.
- A regression bench comes with it, `core_fetchnext_tb`: two faults before
  the fix, one after.
- RD68041's `make check` passes.
- The patch is documented for upstream.

**Results on the Wukong:** all with the SD card untouched (`--io --no-sd`).

| | Userspace | L7 | Login | wget | FPU stress (2 rounds) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MC68040 | RD68031's glibc (L8) | PASS | 37 s | 153 KB/s | PASS, 413 s |
| MC68LC040 | RD68031's musl (L9) | PASS | 29 s | 88 KB/s | PASS, 1945 s |
| MC68040 | 68040 glibc (L16) | PASS | 36 s | 149 KB/s | PASS, 409 s |
| MC68LC040 | 68040 musl (L16) | PASS | 28 s | 89 KB/s | PASS, 1837 s |

The wget rates vary from run to run: 7 KB/s once, 149 the next time, with
the same images. They varied for RD68031 too (28 to 110 KB/s), and the md5s
always matched, so the LAN, not either CPU, seems to set them.

**The SD card:** with the MC68040 and its 68040 buildroot. This wiped the
card's NetBSD filesystems, as allowed.
- `--io`: ext2 on the card, 4 MiB written and read back by md5 after the
  caches were dropped.
- `--sd-install`: p1 FAT (kernel, device tree, litexboot), p2 the glibc
  root, p3 the musl root, every file checked. The first attempt hung on a
  `wget` of `boot.json` and the second passed, so the hang looks transient.
- `--sd-boot`: the BIOS boots from the card, root on p2, to a login.

**In litex_sim:** the 68040 glibc buildroot with the SD emulator, L7 PASS
in 54 minutes (`/proc/cpuinfo`: 68040, FPU 68040).

**RD68031 after all this:** on its own bitstreams, with the kernels that now
have M68040 too, L7 still passes on both. Before that, its bench, sim/unit,
L3, L4 and fpswitch passed after each shared change.

### The 68040 buildroots (L16)

`litex_rd68041_fpu_defconfig` and `litex_rd68041_nofpu_defconfig` are L8's
and L9's with `BR2_m68k_68040`. The soft-float one reuses `patches/gcc` and
`patches/musl` as they are.

`check-softfloat.sh` had one false positive. objdump decodes `$04C4`, data
among busybox's code, as ColdFire's `ff1`, a mnemonic starting with "f".
